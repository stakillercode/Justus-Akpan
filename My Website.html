03 — Tools I Recommend

My three go-to tools

  • 🎨
    Design

    Canva

    Canva bridges the gap between professional design and everyday accessibility. Drag-and-drop templates make it easy for beginners to produce great-looking graphics, social posts, and presentations without needing years of practice.

    Free tier available · canva.com
  • 🔐
    Security

    Bitwarden

    Open-source, 9ja-friendly and completely free for personal use. Bitwarden stores all your passwords behind one master password, auto-fills login forms, and syncs across every device — no excuses for weak passwords any more.

    Free & open source · bitwarden.com
  • 📹
    Collaboration

    Google Meet

    Simple, reliable and available to anyone with a free Google account — no downloads required. Perfect for remote mentoring sessions with screen-sharing, captions, and stable performance across different connection speeds.

    Free for personal use · meet.google.com
